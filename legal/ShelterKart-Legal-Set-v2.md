## 1. Independence and method

**1.1 Operator.** ShelterKart is operated by [Company legal name], [registered address]. Support: [support e-mail]. Grievance Officer: [name], [e-mail], [postal address].

**1.2 Method.** Every report is produced under ShelterKart's published method. The method sets how research is done, which questions are asked, how findings are judged and how the analysis is worked out.

**1.3 No changes for payment or request.** ShelterKart does not change a report, a finding or a question because of a payment, a request, an agreement or a complaint from any person. A change to the method applies to all reports, is dated, and is published with its reason before it takes effect.

**1.4 Sources.** Each finding links to the source it came from and shows the date the source was read. A finding is shown only where a source supports it. Where sources disagree, both are shown. [If applicable: each report prepared by hand is labelled as such.]

**1.5 Who may buy.** Any person or organisation may buy a report, including buyers, property developers, builders, brokers, lawyers, legal institutes, lenders and other institutions. They pay the same price and receive the same report.

**1.6 What a purchase does not give.** A purchase does not give the purchaser any right to change, add or remove a finding or question, to delay a report, or to see a report before it is published.

**1.7 No payment for influence.** ShelterKart does not accept payment to influence the research or the content of any report.

**1.8 Conflicts.** Each owner, director, employee, contractor and adviser of ShelterKart discloses in writing any employment, paid engagement, shareholding or family interest in a company that may be researched. A person with such an interest does not research, write, review or approve a report on that company. ShelterKart keeps a register of disclosures. [Counsel to confirm the disclosure wording and whether any disclosure must be published.]

**1.9 Right of reply.** A party named in a report may respond to a finding. Responses are shown beside the finding they answer, labelled as the party's response, and dated. A response does not change the finding. A correction changes a finding only with evidence, and is dated and public.

## 2. Price, payments and refunds

**2.1 Price.** The price is shown before payment, in the currency shown, including applicable taxes. The price shown at checkout applies for the period stated there.

**2.2 Payment.** Payments are taken by [payment provider]. ShelterKart does not store card details.

**2.3 Delivery.** Reports are delivered to the e-mail address given at checkout and to the buyer's account. Delivery to WhatsApp happens only where the buyer has given a number and asked for it.

**2.4 Refunds for non-delivery or technical faults.** ShelterKart refunds the price in full where:
(a) the report is not delivered within [X] days of payment, unless the delay is caused by the buyer; or
(b) the report cannot be read because of a technical fault, and the fault is not corrected within [X] days of the buyer reporting it.

**2.5 Refunds not given.** A refund is not given because the buyer disagrees with a finding, because a party has responded under clause 1.9, because a finding has been corrected, or because the buyer has not used the report.

**2.6 Refunds and findings.** No refund, credit or payment is linked to changing, removing or softening a finding.

**2.7 Requesting a refund.** Write to [refund e-mail] with the payment reference. ShelterKart replies within [X] working days. Refunds go to the original payment method.

**2.8 Statutory rights.** Nothing in this Part limits any right a consumer has under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

## 3. Terms of use

**3.1 The service.** ShelterKart provides research reports on property projects and developers, compiled from public sources and automated research under the method in Part 1.

**3.2 Information, not advice.** Reports are general information. They are not legal, tax, financial or investment advice, and they do not say whether to buy. You should take advice from a licensed professional before acting.

**3.3 Accuracy.** ShelterKart takes reasonable care in preparing reports. Sources may be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Each finding shows its source and date so that you can check it. ShelterKart does not guarantee that any report is complete.

**3.4 Limit of liability.** To the extent permitted by law, ShelterKart's total liability for a report is limited to the price paid for it. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. [Counsel to confirm the limit and its wording.]

**3.5 Use of the service.** You may use a report for your own decisions and for advice you give in a professional capacity, provided you do not present the report as your own work. You must not harass anyone through the service, scrape the site in bulk, republish a report as your own product, or try to access another person's account or report.

**3.6 Intellectual property.** The site, the method, the design and the reports are protected by intellectual property law. You may keep a report you have bought for your own use. You may not sell, republish or adapt a report commercially without written permission.

**3.7 Account access.** Access to a purchased report depends on the e-mail address and payment reference used at checkout. You are responsible for keeping them secure.

**3.8 Third-party sources.** Reports link to and may quote third-party sources. ShelterKart does not control those sources.

**3.9 Law and courts.** These terms are governed by the laws of India. The courts at [city] have jurisdiction, without affecting any right a consumer has to bring a claim where they live. [Counsel to confirm any pre-action step.]

**3.10 Changes.** ShelterKart may change these terms. The current version and its date are shown on this page. A change does not affect a report already purchased.

## 4. Privacy policy

**4.1 Controller.** [Company legal name] is responsible for personal data processed through ShelterKart. Contact: [privacy e-mail]. Grievance Officer: [name], [e-mail], [postal address].

**4.2 Data we collect.**
(a) At checkout: e-mail address; WhatsApp number, if given; billing country; the property searched; and the payment reference from the payment provider.
(b) On the site: technical data needed to run the service, and usage events (such as a visit or a click). Usage events do not contain personal details. [Confirm against the live code before publication.]
(c) When you contact us: the content of your message and your contact details.
(d) When you respond to or correct a report: your e-mail address (never published), your name or organization if you give one, and your message (published only if it is a reply).
(e) If you ask for your report to be delivered by e-mail: that address, kept with the report’s reference, so we can tell you if a finding in it is corrected. It is used for that one purpose.

ShelterKart does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Do not use the service if you are under 18.

**4.3 Purposes.** To deliver the report you buy; to take payment and issue invoices as tax law requires; to answer enquiries, refunds and corrections; to keep records the law requires; and to keep the service secure and working, using aggregate figures that do not identify you.

**4.4 Consent.** Where we rely on consent, you give it when you enter your details and tick the consent box at checkout. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to [privacy e-mail]. Withdrawal does not affect anything done before it, and where the law permits us to continue without consent, we may do so for the purposes it allows.

**4.5 Messages.** Report delivery and service messages are sent by e-mail and, if you gave a WhatsApp number, by WhatsApp. Marketing messages are sent only with separate consent, which you can withdraw at any time. [Counsel to confirm the consent record.]

**4.6 Recipients.** We share personal data only with: our payment provider, to take payment; our hosting and e-mail providers, to run the service; and authorities where the law requires it. Providers may process data outside India as the law permits, under contract. We do not sell personal data.

**4.7 Retention.** We keep personal data for as long as the purpose requires, and for the periods the law sets. Payment records are kept for [X] years. Data no longer needed is deleted or anonymised.

**4.8 Your rights.** You may ask us to confirm and summarise the personal data we hold about you; correct it; erase it where the law allows; withdraw consent; and nominate another person to exercise your rights if the law allows. Write to [privacy e-mail] or to the Grievance Officer. We respond within [X] days.

**4.9 Complaints.** If you are dissatisfied with our response, contact the Grievance Officer. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is in operation, as the law provides.

**4.10 Security and breaches.** We apply reasonable safeguards, including access controls and encryption in transit. If a personal data breach is likely to cause you harm, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

**4.11 Changes.** We update this policy when our practices change and show its date. We will tell you of a material change before it takes effect.

## 5. Right of reply and corrections

**5.1 Right of reply.** A party named in a report may respond to any finding using the "Respond or correct" link beside it. Before publication, a response is screened automatically for content that is unlawful, such as defamation of a named individual, personal data, or threats; a response that is flagged, and every legal or privacy notice, is looked at by a person before anything is published. The sender must first confirm their e-mail address.

**5.2 Display.** A response appears beside the finding it answers, labelled as the party's response, with its date. It does not replace the finding and does not change any finding.

**5.3 Corrections.** Anyone may report an error in a finding to [corrections e-mail], giving the finding, the reason and any source. ShelterKart acknowledges each correction within [X] working days and decides it within [X] working days.

**5.4 Publishing a correction.** A correction appears next to the report with its date. The original wording stays visible alongside it.

**5.5 No charge.** There is no charge for a response or a correction.

**5.6 Complaints about the method.** Send complaints about the method to [method e-mail]. ShelterKart replies in writing. Any change that follows is published under clause 1.3.
