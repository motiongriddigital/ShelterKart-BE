## 1. Independence and method

**1.1 Who runs ShelterKart.** ShelterKart is operated by [Company legal name], [registered address]. Contact: [support e-mail]. Grievance Officer: [name], [e-mail], [postal address].

**1.2 The method is fixed.** How reports are researched, which questions are asked, how findings are judged and how the analysis is worked out are set by ShelterKart's published method. No buyer, developer, lender, broker, lawyer, institution or other stakeholder can change any part of it, for any payment, under any agreement, or through any refund or complaint.

**1.3 Changes to the method.** A change to the method applies to every report at once. It is dated, published with its reason before it takes effect, and never made for one report or one party.

**1.4 Findings come from sources.** Each finding links to the public source it came from and shows the date that source was read. A finding appears only where a source supports it. Where sources disagree, both are shown. [If applicable: example reports prepared by hand are labelled as such on the report itself.]

**1.5 Who may buy.** Anyone may buy a report, including buyers, property developers, builders, brokers, lawyers, legal institutes, lenders and other institutions, on the same terms and at the same price. No one is refused because of who they are.

**1.6 What buying does not do.** Buying a report gives the buyer no right to change, add, remove, delay or preview any finding, question or analysis. No money is accepted from anyone to influence the research or any report, and no payment can alter the method.

**1.7 Conflicts of interest.** Every owner, director, employee, contractor and adviser of ShelterKart must declare in writing any employment, paid engagement, shareholding or family interest in a company that may be researched. A person with such an interest does not research, write, review or approve any report on that company. ShelterKart keeps a conflicts register, available to counsel and to regulators on request. [If any such interest exists at publication, it is disclosed on this page.]

**1.8 Right of reply.** A party named in a report may respond to any finding. A response is shown beside the finding it answers and is labelled as the party's response. A response does not change the finding.

**1.9 Changes to this page.** This page shows its date of last change. Any change that affects the method is published under clause 1.3.

## 2. Who may buy, payments and refunds

**2.1 Price.** The price of each report is shown before payment, in the currency shown, including applicable taxes. The price is fixed for the period shown on the checkout page.

**2.2 Payments.** Payments are processed by [payment provider], which handles card, UPI and net-banking details. ShelterKart does not store card numbers.

**2.3 Delivery.** Reports are delivered by e-mail and to the buyer's account, and by WhatsApp where the buyer has asked for it. The buyer can download the report as a PDF.

**2.4 When a refund is given.** A full refund is given where:
- the report is not delivered within [X] days of payment, and the delay is not caused by the buyer; or
- the report contains a technical error that prevents it being read, and it is not corrected within [X] days of a request.

**2.5 When a refund is not given.** A refund is not given because a buyer disagrees with a finding, because a developer has replied, because a finding is later corrected, or because the buyer does not use the report.

**2.6 Refunds never change findings.** No refund, credit or payment is linked to the removal, alteration or softening of any finding.

**2.7 How to ask for a refund.** Write to [refunds e-mail] with the payment reference. Refund requests are answered within [X] working days. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

**2.8 Consumer rights.** Nothing in this section limits rights that a buyer has under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

## 3. Terms of use

**3.1 The service.** ShelterKart provides research reports about property projects and developers. Reports are compiled from public sources and automated research under the method in section 1.

**3.2 General information only.** Reports are general information. They are not legal, tax, financial or investment advice. They do not say whether to buy, and they do not replace advice from a licensed professional.

**3.3 Accuracy.** ShelterKart takes reasonable care. Sources can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Each finding shows its source and date so that you can check it. ShelterKart does not guarantee that a report is complete.

**3.4 Limit of liability.** To the extent the law allows, ShelterKart's total liability for any report is limited to the price paid for that report. Nothing in these terms limits any liability that cannot be limited under applicable law, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. [Counsel to confirm the limit under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.]

**3.5 Your use of the service.** You may use the service for your own decisions and for advice you give to others in a professional capacity, if you do not present a report as your own work. You must not:
- use the service to harass anyone;
- scrape or copy the site in bulk;
- republish a report as your own product;
- attempt to gain access to other buyers' reports or accounts.

**3.6 Intellectual property.** The site, the method, the design and the reports are protected by intellectual property law. You may keep a report you have bought for your own use. You may not sell, republish or adapt it commercially without permission.

**3.7 Accounts and access.** Access to a purchased report is tied to the e-mail address and payment reference used at checkout. You are responsible for keeping them secure.

**3.8 Third-party content.** Reports link to third-party sources and may quote them. ShelterKart does not control those sources and is not responsible for their content.

**3.9 Governing law and disputes.** These terms are governed by the laws of India. Courts at [city] have jurisdiction, subject to any right of a consumer to sue where they live. [Counsel to add any mediation step.]

**3.10 Changes to these terms.** These terms may change. The current version and its date are always shown. A change does not affect a report already bought.

## 4. Privacy policy

**4.1 Who we are.** [Company legal name] is the data fiduciary for personal data collected through ShelterKart. Contact: [privacy e-mail]. Grievance Officer: [name], [e-mail], [postal address].

**4.2 What we collect.**
- **When you buy a report:** your e-mail address; your WhatsApp number, if you give it; your billing country; the property you searched for; and the payment reference from our payment provider.
- **When you use the site:** basic technical data needed to run the service, and usage events (such as a visit or a click). Usage events do not store personal details. [Confirm this against the live code before publication.]
- **When you contact us:** the content of your message and your contact details.

We do not collect data from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, do not use the service.

**4.3 Why we use it.**
- to deliver the report you bought;
- to take payment and issue invoices, as the tax law requires;
- to answer your enquiries and handle refunds and corrections;
- to keep records the law requires;
- to keep the service secure and to improve it, using aggregate figures that do not identify you.

**4.4 Consent and notice.** We use your personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you enter your details and tick the consent box at checkout, and for the purposes that the law allows without consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything done before.

**4.5 Messages.** Report delivery and service messages are sent by e-mail and, if you have given your WhatsApp number, by WhatsApp. We send marketing messages only with a separate, recorded consent that you can withdraw at any time. [Counsel to set the consent record and wording.]

**4.6 Who receives your data.** We share personal data only with:
- our payment provider, to take payment;
- our hosting and e-mail providers, to run the service;
- authorities, where the law requires it.

These providers may process data outside India as permitted by law and under contract. We do not sell personal data.

**4.7 How long we keep it.** We keep personal data for as long as needed for the purpose, and for the periods the tax, consumer and other laws require. Records of payments are kept for [X] years. When the period ends, data is deleted or made anonymous.

**4.8 Your rights.** You may ask us:
- for a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
- to correct or update it;
- to erase it, where the law allows;
- to withdraw consent;
- to name someone to act for you if you are unable to act yourself, where the law allows.

To use these rights, write to [privacy e-mail] or to the Grievance Officer. We respond within [X] days.

**4.9 Complaints.** If you are unhappy with our response, contact our Grievance Officer. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational, as the law provides.

**4.10 Security and breaches.** We use reasonable safeguards, including access controls and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

**4.11 Changes.** We will update this policy when our practices change and show the date of the change. For a material change, we will tell you before it takes effect.

## 5. Right of reply and corrections

**5.1 Right of reply.** A party named in a report may respond to any finding through the "Respond or correct" link beside it. Before publication, a response is screened automatically for content that is unlawful, such as defamation of a named individual, personal data or threats; a response that is flagged, and every legal or privacy notice, is looked at by a person before anything is published. The sender must first confirm their e-mail address. ShelterKart does not otherwise edit a response.

**5.2 How a response is shown.** A response appears beside the finding it answers, labelled as the party's response, with its date. It does not replace the finding, and it does not change any finding.

**5.3 Corrections.** If a finding is wrong, anyone may tell us through [corrections e-mail] with the finding, the reason and any source. We acknowledge each correction within [X] working days and decide it within [X] working days.

**5.4 How a correction is published.** A correction is published next to the report, with its date and the original wording preserved. A correction does not hide the original finding.

**5.5 No payment for corrections.** No payment, and no purchase, is needed to make a correction or a response.

**5.6 Complaints about the method.** Complaints about the method go to [method e-mail]. They are answered in writing. Any change they lead to is published under section 1.3.

## 7. Cookies and analytics

**7.1 What we use.** We use cookies and similar storage that are needed to run the site (for example, to keep your language and cookie choice), and simple analytics that count visits and clicks without identifying you. [Confirm the exact list against the live code before publication.]

**7.2 Your choices.** You can clear or block cookies in your browser. Blocking the cookies needed to run the service may stop some features working.

**7.3 Local storage.** Some saved settings and unlocked reports may be kept on your device, so that you can return to them. Clearing your browser data removes them; the report can be recovered from the e-mail and payment reference.

**7.4 Where a visit came from.** While a visit lasts, the page keeps a note of the campaign link or website that brought the visitor. Where the law requires agreement first (visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and any visitor whose country cannot be told), the page asks, with a plain choice of "Allow" or "No thanks", and neither keeps the note on the device nor sends it until the visitor agrees; the visitor's choice is remembered on the device. Elsewhere the note is kept in session storage, which is cleared when the tab is closed and holds no identifier, with notice in the privacy policy. If the visitor buys a report, the note is sent with the order and kept with the purchase record, with the property's market, so that the company can tell which channels lead to purchases.
