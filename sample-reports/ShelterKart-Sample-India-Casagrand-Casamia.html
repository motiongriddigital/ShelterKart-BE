Your protections as a buyer

For India — Tamil Nadu. Verified 2026-09-30. General information about buyers’ rights, not legal advice for your case. Laws change: check the current position with a lawyer before you act.

  • Your right

    Never pay more than 10% before a registered agreement

    The developer may not take more than 10% of the price, as an advance or an application fee, before a written agreement for sale is registered. Anything more before that point is your largest avoidable risk.

    RERA Act 2016, Section 13(1) · Sources: RERA Act 2016, India Code (official) · RERA Act 2016, Legislative Department (official) · BankBazaar

  • Your right

    Late possession: interest for every month, or a full refund

    If possession is not given by the date in your agreement, you may stay and claim interest for every month of delay until possession, or withdraw and claim everything you paid back with interest. State rules set the rate, typically SBI’s marginal cost of lending rate plus 2%. As an illustration only, interest at 10% a year on ₹1 crore is about ₹83,000 for each month of delay.

    RERA Act 2016, Section 18 · Sources: RERA Act 2016, India Code (official) · RERA Act 2016, Legislative Department (official) · LawCrust

  • Your right

    Five years to report defects, fixed free within 30 days

    Structural defects, and defects in workmanship, quality or services, that you report within five years of possession must be fixed by the developer within 30 days at no cost to you.

    RERA Act 2016, Section 14(3) · Sources: RERA Act 2016, India Code (official) · RERA Act 2016, Legislative Department (official) · Lloyd Law College

  • Your right

    70% of your money must stay in the project’s own account

    At least 70% of what buyers pay must be kept in a separate account for that project and used only for its land and construction. Ask which bank holds it, and pay only into that account.

    RERA Act 2016, Section 4(2)(l)(D) · Sources: RERA Act 2016, India Code (official) · RERA Act 2016, Legislative Department (official) · Drishti IAS

  • If it goes wrong

    If something goes wrong: complain to the state RERA

    Any buyer can file a complaint with the state Real Estate Regulatory Authority, online, about any breach of the Act, the rules or the agreement. Keep every receipt, letter and message in writing.

    RERA Act 2016, Section 31 · Sources: RERA Act 2016, India Code (official) · RERA Act 2016, Legislative Department (official) · RERA Property

  • Budget for

    GST on an under-construction home

    An under-construction home carries 5% GST (1% for affordable housing), on top of the price. A completed home with its occupation certificate carries none.

    GST on construction services · Sources: 99acres · The India Post

  • Budget for

    Stamp duty and registration in Tamil Nadu: check the current rates

    Stamp duty and registration are due on top of the price and are not covered by a home loan. ShelterKart could not verify the current Tamil Nadu rates from more than one source, so check them with the state Registration Department (tnreginet.gov.in) before you budget.

    Indian Stamp Act, as applied in Tamil Nadu · Sources: CalcWise (points to tnreginet.gov.in)
