Reading between the lines

  • The 'direct MRT connection' claim appears consistently on agent and marketing microsites but not in the one genuinely independent property-portal source checked (99.co). This is the same agent-microsite pattern this product watches for in every market.

    Can the sales team point to the specific underground link on Great World MRT's own station map?

  • Singapore projects carry two kinds of date. The estimated TOP (2H 2030 here) is a forecast; the statutory dates in the sale documents (vacant possession by 31 December 2032) are the contractual deadlines. Listings quoting July 2029 are wrong on both counts.

    What vacant-possession date is in my Option to Purchase and Sale and Purchase Agreement, and what is the current estimated TOP?

What to ask, and who

Before booking

  • Sales team

    “Is River Modern's seller GuocoLand Limited (UEN 197600660W) directly, or a separate project subsidiary?”

    Good answer: A specific project-company name and UEN, distinct from or matching the parent.

    Red flag: Just ‘GuocoLand’ with no UEN given.

Before signing

  • Your own lawyer

    “What is the Option to Purchase's expiry date, and is my booking fee refundable if I do not exercise it?”

    Good answer: A specific date and a written refund policy for the booking fee itself.

    Red flag: ‘It's standard, don't worry about it.’

Before each payment

  • CRM team

    “Under the Housing Developers Rules, which construction milestone does each payment instalment correspond to?”

    Good answer: A written progressive-payment schedule tied to specific milestones.

    Red flag: A payment request with no milestone stated.

Your protections as a buyer

For Singapore. Verified 2026-09-30. General information about buyers’ rights, not legal advice for your case. Laws change: check the current position with a lawyer before you act.

  • Your right

    You pay as construction progresses

    For a home under construction, payment follows a schedule set by law: 5% booking fee in cash, 15% within eight weeks of the option, then stages tied to construction milestones, with the final 15% due at statutory completion, about a year after the occupation permit. A demand that does not follow it is a red flag.

    Housing Developers (Control and Licensing) Act; Housing Developers Rules · Sources: MoneySmart · Ohmyhome · PropertyNet

  • Your right

    The date that binds is in your agreement

    The estimated completion (TOP) date is a forecast. The date for vacant possession in your Sale and Purchase Agreement is the contractual deadline, and is usually later. Hold the developer to that one.

    Sale and Purchase Agreement (Housing Developers Rules) · Sources: EdgeProp (statutory disclosure) · PropKaki

  • Budget for

    Buyer’s Stamp Duty, and possibly Additional Buyer’s Stamp Duty

    Buyer’s Stamp Duty, in tiers starting at 1%, is due within 14 days of signing. Additional Buyer’s Stamp Duty applies depending on residency and how many homes you own. Use IRAS’s official calculator for your case.

    Stamp Duties Act (IRAS) · Sources: EdgeProp · PropertyNet
