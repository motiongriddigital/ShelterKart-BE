Your protections as a buyer

For United Kingdom. Verified 2026-09-30. General information about buyers’ rights, not legal advice for your case. Laws change: check the current position with a lawyer before you act.

  • Your right

    14 days to change your mind after reserving

    If you reserve a new-build home and change your mind within 14 days, the builder must refund your reservation fee in full. After that, any deduction must be limited to reasonable costs stated to you up front.

    Consumer Code for Home Builders; New Homes Quality Code · Sources: Consumer Code for Home Builders · Boyes Turner · HCR Law

  • Your right

    Your deposit is protected if the builder fails

    The codes require builders to have deposit protection, so your money is covered if the builder goes out of business before your home is completed. Ask which scheme protects your deposit.

    Consumer Code for Home Builders · Sources: Consumer Code for Home Builders · Consumer Code media sheet

  • Your right

    A ten-year warranty, and two years of builder liability

    Virtually every new home has a ten-year structural warranty (NHBC, LABC Warranty or Premier Guarantee). Builders under the New Homes Quality Code also owe a two-year liability period for defects. Ask which warranty provider covers your home.

    New home warranties; New Homes Quality Code · Sources: Consumer Code for Home Builders · HCR Law

  • Your right

    Inspect before completion, and see the running costs

    Under the New Homes Quality Code you may inspect the home before completion, and the builder must tell you about management and service charges you could face in the first ten years.

    New Homes Quality Code · Sources: HCR Law · Gowling WLG

  • If it goes wrong

    If something goes wrong: the New Homes Ombudsman

    Complain to the builder first. If it is not resolved, and the builder is registered under the New Homes Quality Code, you can take it to the New Homes Ombudsman Service (after 56 days). Otherwise, start with your warranty provider.

    New Homes Quality Code · Sources: Gowling WLG · Boyes Turner

  • Budget for

    Stamp Duty Land Tax

    Stamp Duty Land Tax is due on completion, at rates that depend on the price and whether you own other homes. ShelterKart does not quote the rates here: use HMRC’s official SDLT calculator.

    Stamp Duty Land Tax (HMRC) · Sources: Consumer Code media sheet
