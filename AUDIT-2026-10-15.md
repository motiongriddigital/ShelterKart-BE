# Quality audit, 15 October 2026

## How it was checked
- **Website (V2), all 9 languages at phone and desktop width:** script errors, sideways overflow, duplicate IDs, broken in-page links,
  unlabelled buttons, images without descriptions, and any visible text still in English where a translation exists, through the whole
  journey (home, investigation, preview, checkout, after payment).
- **Reports, previews, sample pages and the start page:** scanned for "undefined", "NaN", "null", unfilled {placeholders}, empty sections.
- **Investor deck:** every headline number checked against the model and the live facts (`deck/check_deck_v2.py`); overflow on every slide.
- **All test suites:** 681 checks in 20 suites, recorded by `tools/count_checks.py`.

## Found and fixed
1. **Country names were English in every other language** (the 36 market buttons, the hidden list, the market count). They now follow the
   reader's language, and are redrawn when it changes (भारत, الهند, Inde, 印度, インド...).
2. **An English report inside an Arabic page ran right to left** (".to ask before you pay", "October 2026 13"). It is now always left to
   right and marked English.
3. **The sample links and checkout title showed English place names** ("Mumbai, India"); now translated.
4. **The investor deck quoted stale counts** ("eight languages", "673 checks"). Every count is now read from the code and a real test run.
5. **The release notes quoted 669 checks**, and the versions note an old language count; corrected.
6. A cosmetic step that, in an unusual browser, could have stopped the page starting; now it can never block start-up.

Each fix is covered by a test or by the audit scripts, so it cannot silently come back.

## Not a defect
- The console shows one failed request offline: Google Fonts, which loads normally online.
- "ShelterKart · Evidence Standard" on the seal stays English on purpose: it is the brand mark.
- Reports are English in every language; the site says so.

## What only you can close (no amount of building here can)
1. **The first live run** with real AI keys: the only way to know real accuracy and cost per report.
2. **A native-speaker check** of the eight translations (all are marked AI-checked, truthfully).
3. **Legal review** of the methodology, terms and buyer-protection wording, by a lawyer per market before launch there.
4. **Live payments and delivery:** Razorpay activation, the e-mail provider, a Meta-approved WhatsApp template.
5. **Real users:** conversion and cost to win a buyer are assumptions until a closed beta measures them.
6. **Before investors see the deck:** convert the OPC, confirm the stipend, retainer and insurance quotes, and add your story to the team slide.
