Pricing

Searching is free, and every search shows a free preview: the project, the first findings, the topics sources disagree on, and what could not be found.

The full report adds every finding with its sources, what each disagreement actually says, the questions to ask and who to ask, the buyer’s protections, every page checked, and a check of your own documents against the report.

How the price of a report is set

Each report has one price, set by code from two things, and it is the same for every buyer, wherever they are:

  • The value of the property. The price follows the midpoint of the project’s own listed prices: a report on a more expensive home costs more, but less as a share of the home’s price. Where no listed price is found, the report is priced as a typical purchase (about $161,017).
  • The depth of the evidence: Gold standard (high confidence, including an official source) 100%; Heavy (much of the evidence corroborated) 80%; Thin (a thin public record) 60%. ShelterKart decides this from the evidence; the buyer never chooses it.

Your country decides only the currency you pay in, the tax, and how you can pay. The price is read from a published list of round prices for your currency, so it does not move with daily exchange rates. When you unlock a report, your price is locked for 30 minutes. If your billing country turns out to be different, you see the new total and confirm it before anything is charged.

The price never depends on whether the findings favor the developer.

Tax

Prices include tax where ShelterKart collects it: in India, GST. Where ShelterKart is not registered for a country’s tax, no tax is charged, and the checkout says so. The checkout always shows the report fee, the tax and the total as separate lines.
