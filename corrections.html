Corrections

If something in a report is wrong, tell us. Every correction request is checked against the original source the finding cites.

How to report an error

Use Report an inaccuracy on any report, or write to corrections@shelterkart.com with the report’s reference (it starts with SK-) and what is wrong.

What happens next

We reply within 2 business days. If the source does not say what the report says, the finding is corrected or removed; if sources genuinely disagree, both versions are shown. Submitting a request does not by itself change a finding: the source decides. A paid finding that is shown to be wrong against its own source, and is not corrected within 7 days, is refunded under our refund policy.

Developers’ right of reply

Any developer can reply to a finding about it, free and unconditionally. Replies are reviewed and published alongside the finding they respond to, never in place of it. A reply cannot change a finding; a correction to the underlying source can.
