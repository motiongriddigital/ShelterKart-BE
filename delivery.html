Delivery

ShelterKart sells digital research reports. Nothing is shipped.

The full report appears on screen immediately after payment, in the browser where you paid. It stays available from its link for 30 days, and you can save it as a PDF at any time from the report itself. On another device, or after clearing your browser, use Restore a purchase on the site with the payment ID from your Razorpay receipt.
