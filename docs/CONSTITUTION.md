# The ShelterKart Constitution
### One page. Not ten volumes. If it needs more than a page, it won't get followed.

---

**We don't sell certainty. We reduce uncertainty.**

That sentence is the whole company. Everything below just enforces it.

## The rules that don't change

1. **Trust cannot be purchased.** No developer, at any tier, at any
   price, can raise, lower, or hide a finding or its mark.
2. **Every statement is one of four things, and the reader always knows
   which**: a **Fact** (a specific public filing, cited), an
   **Inference** (something reasonably concluded from facts, labeled as
   such), an **Opinion** (a judgement about what the findings mean — explicitly "an informational
   opinion, not a guarantee"), or a **Prediction** (the possession
   outlook — always hedged, never a promise).
3. **If the evidence isn't there, the answer is "insufficient public
   data," not a guess.** An AI-shaped sentence that sounds confident is
   not evidence. Confidence is never manufactured to fill a gap.
4. **Corrections are never silent.** What changed, when, and why is
   always visible — never a quietly edited report.
5. **Developers are a stakeholder, not a target.** A fair finding beats a
   flattering one. The only way to improve how a project reads is to improve the
   underlying public record — never to pay.
6. **AI may**: search, extract, summarize, translate, compare,
   cross-check, draft. **AI may never**: invent a fact, originate a
   claim with no source behind it, or publish without the gating logic
   in `server.js` (cache → cheap recheck → only then, synthesis) being
   satisfied first.
7. **Scope stays scope.** New-build, primary sales, direct from
   developer. Not resale, not secondary market, not land — until a
   deliberate decision changes that, not by accident.

## What this protects

Every one of these lines exists because it closes a specific real risk
already discussed and reasoned through in this project: defamation
exposure (rules 2, 3), the "pay to fix a finding" extortion-adjacent
failure mode (rules 1, 5), and the credibility a correction process
depends on (rule 4). None of it is decoration.

## The one test for anything new

Before adding a feature, a country, or an AI workflow, ask: **does this
make a specific statement more traceable to evidence, or does it just
make the product look more impressive?** If it's the second one without
also being the first, it doesn't belong yet.
