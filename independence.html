Independence

This text is available in English only. The English version governs.

No developer can pay to add, change, or remove any finding, question, or part of a report.

Every claim on a report traces to a specific public source, graded by how well it is supported: confirmed at an official source, reported by several independent sites, resting on a single source, or the developer’s own claim. We never present an unverified claim with the confidence of a verified one.

Where we don’t have reliable public data, we say so plainly rather than filling the gap with a plausible-sounding guess.

Payment unlocks the full findings and the source trail. It never changes a finding.

ShelterKart is paid by buyers, for reports. A report’s price depends on the project’s listed price and the depth of the evidence; your country decides only the currency and the tax — never on whether the findings favor the developer.
